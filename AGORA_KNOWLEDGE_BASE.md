# THE HIMALAYAN VISTA, MUSSOORIE - OFFICIAL HOTEL KNOWLEDGE BASE

## 1. HOTEL GENERAL INFORMATION
- **Hotel Name**: The Himalayan Vista
- **Tagline**: Luxury Heritage & Valley Retreat • Est. 1912
- **Location**: Library End, Camel's Back Road, Mussoorie, Uttarakhand 248179, India
- **Elevation**: 6,580 feet overlooking Doon Valley and Himalayan Snow Peaks
- **Reception & Concierge**: 24 Hours Open (Dial 100 from room intercom or call +91 135 263 5890)
- **Check-in Time**: 2:00 PM (14:00 IST)
- **Check-out Time**: 11:00 AM (11:00 IST)
- **Early Check-in**: Complimentary from 10:00 AM onwards subject to room availability; before 10:00 AM incurs 50% half-day tariff.
- **Late Check-out**: Complimentary until 1:00 PM upon prior request subject to room availability; check-out until 6:00 PM incurs 50% room tariff; past 6:00 PM incurs full day charge.
- **Wi-Fi**: Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi (up to 200 Mbps). Network SSID: `HimalayanVista_Guest`. Login portal requires Room Number and Guest Last Name.
- **Parking & Valet**: Free secure on-site parking for up to 45 vehicles with 24x7 complimentary valet parking. Features two Type-2 22kW EV charging stations.

## 2. ROOMS & FACILITIES
- **Total Rooms**: 40 luxury valley-facing rooms across 4 floors:
  - Floor 1: Ground/Garden Wing (Heritage Deluxe King/Twin - ₹4,800 to ₹5,200)
  - Floor 2: Valley View Floor (Executive Valley King/Twin - ₹6,200 to ₹6,600)
  - Floor 3: Imperial Suite Wing (Imperial Royal Suite - ₹9,500)
  - Floor 4: Presidential & Penthouse Tier (Maharaja Penthouse & Presidential Sky Villa - ₹13,500 to ₹24,000)
- **Indoor Swimming Pool**: Located on Lower Ground Level (-1). Temperature-controlled glasshouse heated pool with valley views. Open 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Complimentary towels and robes provided. Proper swimwear mandatory.
- **Fitness Studio & Yoga**: Level 1. Open 24/7 with LifeFitness treadmills, ellipticals, weights. Daily guided morning yoga on outdoor deck at 7:00 AM.
- **Climate Control & Heating**: All rooms have centralized dual-cycle inverter climate control plus supplementary radiant oil heaters during winter. Thermostat controls are on bedside console.
- **Banquet & Meetings**: Pine View Banquet Hall seats up to 100 guests with 4K laser projection and valley break-out lawns.

## 3. DINING & RESTAURANTS
- **Pine & Mist Restaurant**: Fine dining multi-cuisine restaurant located on the Ground Floor. Open daily from 7:30 AM to 11:00 PM.
  - Breakfast Buffet: 7:30 AM to 10:30 AM (Complimentary for resident guests). Includes live North & South Indian counters, bakery, Continental dishes, and organic Himalayan honey.
  - Lunch: 12:30 PM to 3:30 PM.
  - Dinner: 7:30 PM to 10:45 PM. Features Garhwali regional delicacies and Awadhi grills.
- **Vegetarian & Jain Dining**: Separate dedicated cookware used for Pure Vegetarian and Jain meals. Jain breakfast items (poha, parathas without onion/garlic, fruit platters) available daily upon request. Gluten-free options available.
- **Sunset High Tea**: Complimentary traditional high tea on Sunset Cedar Lawn between 4:30 PM and 6:00 PM daily with hot masala chai, Darjeeling green tea, pakoras, and scones.
- **24/7 In-Room Dining**: Dial 105 from room intercom. Full menu during daytime; late-night menu (sandwiches, hot parathas, biryani, soups, beverages) available from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM.

## 5. HOTEL POLICIES
- **Cancellation Policy**: 100% refund for cancellations made 48 hours or more prior to 2:00 PM check-in date. Cancellations within 48 hours incur a 1-night room tariff. Peak holiday dates (Dec 23 - Jan 2) non-refundable within 14 days.
- **Smoking Policy**: 100% Non-Smoking property in all indoor rooms, balconies, and corridors (including vapes/e-cigarettes). A ₹5,000 ionization cleaning charge applies for room smoking. Smoking permitted only in designated outdoor gazebo on Pine Lawn.
- **Pet Policy**: Pets are strictly not permitted on premises due to high-altitude wildlife ridge conservation. Certified service dogs require 72h advance management notice.
- **ID Requirement**: Mandatory government photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID) required for all adult guests at check-in. PAN cards are not valid proof of identity/address.
- **Visitors Policy**: Non-resident visitors permitted in lobby and restaurant until 8:00 PM. Overnight guests must register at Front Desk with photo ID.
- **Children**: Children aged 5 and under stay complimentary using existing bedding. Baby cots/cribs provided complimentary upon request.

## 6. LOCAL SIGHTSEEING & TRANSPORTATION
- **Mussoorie Mall Road & Library Chowk**: 2.2 km from hotel (25-minute scenic walk along Camel's Back Road or 7-minute taxi ride).
- **Kempty Falls**: 14 km (approx. 40 minutes by cab). Best visited 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Travel desk arranges private round-trip cab for ₹1,800.
- **Lal Tibba & Landour Bakehouse**: 4.8 km away (7,160 ft elevation). Highest point in Mussoorie with Char Dukan historic tea stalls.
- **Airport & Railway**: Dehradun Jolly Grant Airport (DED) is 60 km away (approx. 2 hours). Dehradun Railway Station is 34 km away (approx. 1 hour 15 minutes). Travel desk arranges one-way airport sedan for ₹2,800.
- **Nearest Hospitals & ATMs**:
  - Civil Hospital Mussoorie: 2.8 km (24x7 Emergency: +91 135 263 2841)
  - Apollo Pharmacy: Kulri Bazaar (2.4 km, open 24 hours)
  - SBI & HDFC ATMs: Library Chowk (2.1 km)
